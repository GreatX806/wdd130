About The Temple

The Lagos Nigeria Temple is the 142nd operating temple of The Church of Jesus Christ of First Presidency announced on April 6, 2013, that a temple would be built in Lagos, Nigeria.

Dedicated: August 7 2005 by Gordon B. Hinckley
