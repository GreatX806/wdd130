History

Cascade Rafting Co. launched with a single raft, a battered pickup truck, and a stubborn belief that the river teaches things a classroom never could. What began as weekend trips for friends has grown into a full outfitter with certified guides, a fleet of rafts, and a basecamp full of regulars who now bring their own kids down the same rapids. The name has changed hands twice, but the mission has not: get people onto moving water, safely, and let the current do the rest.
